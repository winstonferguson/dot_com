Make it right.

How many times does a custom job get re-typed between the quote and production?

I help <%= site.metadata.niche %> businesses find where quoting and hand-off lose time, then build the fixes.

The audit

A fixed-price look at how you quote a job and pass it on to be made. You get a ranked list of what's worth fixing, and an honest view of which fixes need software and which don't.

The fixes

I build the tools the audit points to: pricing and quoting tools, product configurators, order data that reaches production without being typed again. Built in Ruby on Rails, around the systems you already use.

Where AI fits

Sometimes AI is part of the tool, such as turning a customer's description into configurator settings. More often it makes building the tool faster and cheaper.

About me

I'm an operator and manager who moved into software. I've consulted for businesses in retail, aviation and hospitality, and I run my own businesses. My background is in CAD and design, and I founded Saw & Sew, an online mass-customisation furniture business with outsourced production. Now I build custom software in Ruby on Rails and Bridgetown.

I'm also the founder of Datafurn, software for CNC manufacturers. It's in development and recruiting for its Stage 1 pilot.
