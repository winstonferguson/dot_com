The audit

I work with <%= site.metadata.segment %> to follow how work moves through the business, from the first enquiry to the paid invoice. How many times does the same order get typed? Quoting is a common example: a quote is written, then copied into a job sheet, then into an invoice.

You get a ranked list of fixes, the most useful first. Some need software. Many are a better spreadsheet, a template or a change in who does what.

The fixes

If you want them, I build the fixes that need software: quoting and pricing tools, order systems, documents generated from data you already have, and information that moves between your systems without being typed again. Built in Ruby on Rails, around the tools you already use.

Where AI fits

Sometimes AI is part of a fix, such as turning a customer's description into product settings. More often it makes building the fix faster and cheaper.

About me

I'm an operator and manager who moved into software, and I run my own businesses. I've consulted for retail, aviation and hospitality businesses, and since 2017 I've done admin and management consulting for London hospitality businesses, including a financial model and standard operating procedures. My background is in CAD and design: I founded Saw & Sew, an online mass-customisation furniture business with outsourced production, and now I build custom software in Ruby on Rails and Bridgetown.

I'm also the founder of Datafurn, software for CNC manufacturers. It's in development.
