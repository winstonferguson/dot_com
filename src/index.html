What I do

I start with a fixed-price review. I follow a job through your business, from the first enquiry to the paid invoice, and note where it gets typed again, checked twice or waits on someone. You get a short written report with the changes in order of usefulness. Many are a better spreadsheet, a template or a change in who does what.

About

I moved into software through CAD and design and now build with Ruby on Rails. I founded Saw & Sew, an online furniture business, and I'm building Datafurn, software for CNC manufacturers.
