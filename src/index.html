What I do

I start with a fixed-price review. I look at cashflow, build or check the financial model, and follow a job through your business from the first enquiry to the paid invoice. You get a short written report: where to focus first, then the changes in order of usefulness.

Then I can build the systems that fix what the review finds: a quoting tool, an order system, documents generated from data you already hold. Many fixes need no software at all. AI sometimes has a place. More often it just makes the build quicker.

I give operational and financial-modelling advice. I don't give insolvency, legal or tax advice. If you need it, I'll say so and point you to someone qualified.

About

I started in operations at Roche Bobois's North American headquarters. As liaison manager I rolled out its intranet across the North American market, trained operations teams, and then consulted on operations, including sales turnarounds and takeovers. I went on to open and run my own restaurant for seven years. Since 2017 I've done admin and management consulting for London hospitality, including a financial model and standard operating procedures. I moved into software through CAD and design, and now build with Ruby on Rails. I founded Saw & Sew, an online furniture business, and I'm building Datafurn, software for CNC manufacturers.
