What I do

I start with a fixed-price review. I follow money and work through your business: where cash is tight, where jobs get typed again or wait on someone, where the same decision gets made twice. You get a short written report with the changes in order of usefulness. Many are a better process, a template or a change in who does what.

About

I started in operations, then opened and ran my own restaurant for seven years. I've advised London hospitality on admin, finance and management since 2017. I moved into software through CAD and design, and I founded Saw & Sew, an online furniture business. I'm now building Datafurn, software for CNC manufacturers.
